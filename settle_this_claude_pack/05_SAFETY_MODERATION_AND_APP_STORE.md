# Safety, Moderation, and App Store Compliance

## Strategic recommendation

Launch as **18+**, PG default, private-first, no public feed, no NSFW mode.

This gives the best chance of App Store approval and lowers moderation burden.

## App Store-safe product positioning

Use:

- “Funny AI referee”
- “Settle everyday disagreements”
- “Get a fair verdict and practical next step”
- “Private by default”
- “For entertainment and perspective”

Avoid:

- “Therapy”
- “Legal mediator”
- “AI judge for serious conflict”
- “NSFW”
- “Savage” as primary store copy
- “Brutally honest” as primary store copy
- “Anonymous confessions”
- “Expose them”
- “Win every argument”

## Required disclaimers

Place in onboarding, settings, and footer of serious responses:

```text
Settle This is for entertainment and general perspective on everyday low-stakes disagreements. It is not therapy, legal advice, medical advice, crisis support, or a substitute for professional help.
```

For serious content soft redirect:

```text
This sounds bigger than a lighthearted everyday disagreement. Settle This can help you organize your thoughts, but this may deserve a real conversation with trusted support or a qualified professional.
```

## Content categories

### Allowed MVP categories

- Chores
- Laundry
- Cleaning
- Pets
- Food/leftovers
- Scheduling
- Texting etiquette
- Minor relationship annoyances
- Roommate issues
- Family etiquette
- Gift etiquette
- Parenting logistics without abuse/safety issues
- Social plans
- Household spending disagreements without financial/legal advice

### Soft redirect categories

Return a serious, non-funny response:

- Possible emotional abuse
- Major relationship crisis
- Boundary issues that sound coercive
- Potential workplace HR issues
- Legal disputes
- Medical decisions
- Mental health distress
- Severe conflict
- Alcohol/drug-related safety issues
- Parenting issues involving child safety

### Block categories

Do not generate a verdict:

- Self-harm instructions
- Threats or violence
- Sexual content involving minors
- Explicit sexual content
- Doxxing
- Harassment campaigns
- Blackmail
- Revenge
- Stalking
- Criminal evasion
- Hate speech
- Requests to humiliate or manipulate

## Humor rules

Humor should target:

- The situation
- The absurdity
- The household chaos
- The “case” itself

Humor should not target:

- Body, race, gender, sexuality, disability, religion, nationality
- Mental health
- Trauma
- Poverty
- Age
- A named private person
- Someone’s parenting in a cruel way

## Tone mode rules

### Balanced Referee

Safe for all users. This is default.

Allowed language:

- “You were mostly reasonable.”
- “She had a valid point.”
- “Both sides are understandable.”
- “The practical fix is…”

### Playful Roast

Keep it teasing, not cruel.

Good:

- “Mildly guilty of trusting the washing machine with too much responsibility.”
- “The shirt needed a private spa day.”

Bad:

- “You’re an idiot.”
- “Your partner is crazy.”
- “Dump them.”

### Courtroom Judge

Must feel fake/playful.

Good:

- “In the matter of The Cat Pee Laundry Trial…”

Bad:

- “As a legal authority…”
- “You are liable…”

## App review notes

Prepare reviewer notes:

```text
Settle This is a private entertainment app that uses AI to generate lighthearted opinions about low-stakes everyday disagreements. It does not provide therapy, legal, medical, or crisis advice. The app includes safety filtering that blocks or redirects serious topics such as self-harm, abuse, threats, explicit sexual content, legal issues, and medical issues. There is no public feed or anonymous messaging in the MVP.
```

## Privacy posture

MVP should support:

- Delete individual case
- Delete all saved cases
- Delete account
- No public sharing unless user actively shares a card
- No public profiles
- No follower/friend graph
- No user search

## PII handling

Client UI should warn:

```text
Please avoid full names, addresses, phone numbers, workplaces, schools, or other identifying details.
```

Backend should:

- Detect likely phone/email/address patterns
- Flag `containsPotentialPII`
- Remove PII from share card text
- Avoid using real names in generated verdicts

## Sharing rules

Share cards must:

- Avoid real names
- Avoid private identifying facts
- Avoid severe accusations
- Stay PG
- Include small footer: “Generated by Settle This”

Do not allow share card creation for:

- Soft redirect cases
- Blocked cases
- Medium/high-risk sensitive cases
- Cases with detected PII unless redacted

## Reporting and feedback

Even without public feed, include:

- “Report unsafe verdict” button
- “Too harsh” feedback tag
- “Not fair” feedback tag
- “Not helpful” feedback tag

This helps improve prompt safety and shows responsible design.
