# Product Brief — Settle This

## One-liner

**Settle This is a funny, fair AI referee that helps people resolve everyday disagreements with a verdict, practical advice, and a shareable final ruling.**

## Core positioning

The app should be entertainment-first and practical-second.

It should not present itself as:

- Therapy
- Legal mediation
- Medical advice
- A replacement for professionals
- A social shaming platform
- A public confessions forum

It should present itself as:

- A lighthearted dispute referee
- A private AI judge for low-stakes everyday disagreements
- A tool for getting perspective, a laugh, and a next step

## Target users

### Primary persona: Petty Couple

People in relationships who have recurring silly disputes:

- Laundry
- Dishes
- Parenting chores
- Pet messes
- Texting etiquette
- Food leftovers
- In-laws/family plans
- Sleeping temperature
- Household cleanliness

Needs:

- A neutral opinion
- A funny verdict
- Something they can text/share
- Practical advice that does not escalate things

### Secondary persona: Roommates/Friends

People who disagree about chores, money splitting, food, noise, parties, pets, or shared items.

Needs:

- Quick tie-breaker
- Less awkward confrontation
- Shareable result

### Tertiary persona: Family Group Chat

Families arguing over etiquette, parenting boundaries, gifts, events, holiday plans, and fairness.

Needs:

- PG language
- Calm advice
- Funny line that does not humiliate anyone

## Core promise

The app should answer:

- Who was more reasonable?
- What did each side get right?
- What did each side miss?
- What should they do now?
- What is the funniest fair ruling?

## MVP principles

1. **Fast first verdict** — user should get a result within 60–90 seconds of opening the app for the first time.
2. **Structured output** — no generic chatbot wall of text.
3. **Private by default** — no public feed.
4. **Safe humor** — funny but not cruel.
5. **Always useful** — every verdict includes a practical next step.
6. **App Store safe** — PG default, optional PG-13 later, no NSFW branding.
7. **Low stakes only** — serious safety/legal/medical/abuse issues get redirected.

## MVP feature set

### Must have

- User onboarding
- Anonymous trial mode or lightweight auth
- Auth upgrade path
- Submit dispute form
- Tone selector
- AI safety gate
- AI verdict generation
- Verdict result screen
- Save/share verdict
- Feedback buttons
- Usage limits
- Basic analytics
- Privacy/disclaimer screens

### Should have

- Apple Sign In
- Google Sign In
- Email/password sign in
- RevenueCat subscription scaffolding
- History screen
- Shareable image card
- Remote config for prompt/tone tweaks

### Later, not MVP

- Public feed
- Community voting
- Voice recordings
- Screenshot analysis
- Couple invite links
- Judge persona packs
- Video verdict narration
- Creator packs
- Localization

## Competitive differentiation

Compared with generic ChatGPT:

- Faster, structured, no prompt engineering
- Shareable cards
- Prebuilt tone modes
- Guardrails for relationship disputes
- Repeatable fun interaction loop

Compared with AITA/Reddit:

- Private
- Instant
- No public pile-on
- More constructive
- No long waiting or moderation rules

Compared with relationship apps:

- Funnier
- Less serious
- Less therapy-coded
- More viral

Compared with direct AI judge apps:

- Better App Store safety posture
- Better structured practical advice
- More polished brand
- Better share loop
- Less harsh/savage positioning

## Product thesis

The app wins if it becomes the fastest way to turn an everyday argument into:

1. A fair ruling
2. A laugh
3. A next step
4. A shareable quote
