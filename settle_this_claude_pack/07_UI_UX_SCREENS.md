```text

```

Body:

```text
Get a funny, fair AI verdict for everyday disagreements — laundry, pets, chores, leftovers, texting, and all the tiny chaos of real life.
```

Title:

```text
A referee, not a therapist.
```

Body:

```text
Settle This is for low-stakes everyday disagreements. It gives perspective, a practical next step, and a little humor.
```

Title:


Body:

```text
This app does not provide therapy, legal, medical, emergency, or crisis advice. Serious or unsafe topics will get a calm redirect instead of a funny verdict.
```
