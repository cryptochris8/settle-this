# Branding and ASO

## Name recommendation

**Settle This** is still the best working name from a brand clarity standpoint.

However, market research found a direct competitor named **Let’s Settle This**. Before locking the final brand, run trademark and App Store name checks.

## App description draft

```text
Settle This is a funny, fair AI referee for everyday disagreements.

Arguing about laundry? Dishes? Leftovers? Pets? Texting etiquette? Roommate chores? Tell Settle This what happened, choose a tone, and get a quick verdict with both sides explained, a practical next step, and a final ruling you can actually laugh about.

Choose your vibe:
• Balanced Referee — fair, calm, and useful
• Playful Roast — funny without being mean
• Courtroom Judge — tiny gavel energy for everyday chaos

Every verdict includes:
• A clear ruling
• What each side got right
• What everyone missed
• A practical fix
• A funny final line
• A text you can send
• A shareable verdict card

Settle This is built for low-stakes everyday disagreements. It is not therapy, legal advice, medical advice, or crisis support. Serious or unsafe topics are redirected instead of joked about.
```

## Brand voice

The app should sound:

- Fair
- Funny
- Warm
- Specific
- Practical
- A little dramatic in courtroom mode

The app should not sound:

- Cruel
- Clinical
- Overly therapeutic
- Legalistic
- Sexually explicit
- Mean-spirited

## Logo/icon direction

Visual ideas:

- Small gavel with speech bubble
- Smiling courthouse icon
- Scales of justice made playful, not official
- Verdict stamp
- Two chat bubbles with a tiny gavel between them

Avoid:

- Realistic courthouse seal
- Anything too legal/official
- Angry faces
- Anonymous mask imagery
- Dark scandal/confession vibe
